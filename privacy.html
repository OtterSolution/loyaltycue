Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

My Loyalty Cards is designed with privacy in mind. The app does not require an account and is intended to keep your loyalty card information and personalization data on your device.

Information stored by the app

The app may store information that you provide, such as loyalty or membership card names, card codes, colors, images, notes, and app settings. This information is stored locally on your device.

Location and smart suggestions

If you enable location-based suggestions and grant location permission, the app may use your location when you use a card to improve future card suggestions. This information is used for the app's on-device smart-suggestion feature and is intended to remain on your device.

Location-based suggestions are optional. You can disable them and clear learned location data from within the app.

Data collection and tracking

My Loyalty Cards does not use your card information or location history for advertising or cross-app tracking. The app does not require you to create an account.

Backups and exports

If the app allows you to export or back up your data, files you choose to create or share are handled according to the destination you select. You are responsible for where exported files are stored or shared.

Third-party services

The app is designed to operate without an advertising network or a developer-operated backend. Apple system services may be involved when you use operating-system features such as location permissions, file sharing, backups, or other iOS integrations. Those services are governed by their respective terms and privacy practices.

Children's privacy

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated service.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact information provided on the Support page.
